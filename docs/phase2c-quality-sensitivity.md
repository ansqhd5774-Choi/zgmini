## Calculations and comparisons

- L0 retains inverse P252 of daily liquidation amounts.
- L3/L5 calculate arithmetic means over the current and previous 2/4 observations,
  **before** applying inverse P252. No centered averages, score smoothing or new clipping.
- All 3/5 inputs must be finite. Zero is valid. A null remains in its date slot and
  invalidates every dependent mean/percentile window; it is never filled or deleted.
- First usable indices are 251, 253, 255 (zero-based) for L0/L3/L5. Full 252 windows
  are required; no shorter fallback. The mean divides terms before summation to
  avoid unnecessary overflow. Tests independently use a summed arithmetic mean.
- The only new composite experiment is 3 liquidation choices × 2 spread choices
  (P252 or Z252), with LEV_BLEND20_60 fixed and exact equal thirds. No new grid.
- Own-period quality and group-intersection quality have separate start/end/count,
  group and alignment fields. Legacy candidates compare within their axis; all
  legacy models compare together; smoothing and the six combinations have their
  own groups. An empty intersection returns empty metrics, not a substitute period.
- Excluded comparison dates are nulled, not removed, before change/autocorrelation
  metrics. A long gap between genuine successive observations remains a gap, not
  calendar-day adjacency. Yearly summaries restrict the calendar year then apply
  the same group's intersection; year-boundary jumps are excluded from that year's metric.
- Six-combination outputs include distribution, jumps, saturation, threshold
  frequencies, same-date component Pearson/Spearman and per-year counterparts.

## Jump explanation

A/B/C retain their own top 10 absolute adjacent valid score changes. Each reports
dates, calendar gap, scores, signed composite change and signed weighted component
contributions. Absolute residual between their sum and the composite change must
be <= `1e-9` points; otherwise analysis fails closed. Contributions are not rounded.
Missing score slots break pairs. Six combinations report top 10 on common dates;
their yearly rows include the largest eligible interval.

If a component accounts for >=80% of total **absolute** contributions it is marked
single-component-dominant; otherwise multiple contributors. This identifies score
arithmetic, not economic causality. Calendar gaps are flagged without calling them
data loss. Endpoint raw-value checks disclose only zero/invalid-or-missing booleans,
not financial values. Zero is never invalidated simply for being zero. These checks
do not inspect every historical input in the normalization window or confirm a
source-format root cause; `rawInputCausality` remains UNCONFIRMED.

## Signal loss and delay

Raw L0 crossings from >=20 to <20 define reference events. Threshold 20 is a fixed
research setting, not market truth. L3/L5 search at most 20 subsequent **raw-series
observation slots**, fixed before evaluation. Events can overlap; each raw crossing
is independently counted. Already-below, same-observation entry, delayed entry,
complete-horizon non-entry, missing evaluation and right-censored tails are distinct.
The categories sum to eventCount. Missing raw/smoothed values stop evaluation;
they are not silently skipped. Delay histogram and mean are conditional on detected
entries/already-below events, with zero lag for already-below; misses and censored
events are not assigned zero lag. Calendar-day lag is reported separately.
This is `signalLagRelativeToRaw`, never market prediction accuracy or lead evidence.

## Coverage and timing

Missing primary reasons are exclusive, in this precedence:
SOURCE_HISTORY_NOT_STARTED → WARMUP → CURRENT_INPUT_LAG_OR_MISSING →
MISSING_COMPONENT_AFTER_WARMUP → OTHER_UNRESOLVED.
Structural warm-up uses observed source slots including null positions. A complete
window still must be valid for a score, so post-warm-up window contamination is
missing-component-after-warm-up. No-history sources outrank warm-up.

Coverage denominator is the observed union of component dates in the requested
history, separately before/after all sources' structural warm-up ends. The request
start/end are reported by the internal caller. An absent release calendar prevents
an expected-calendar-observation denominator; `requestedCalendarCoverage` explicitly
remains unassessed. Do not interpret weekends or absent dates as API failures.
Publication delay is not established by latestLagDays or generatedAt.
`pointInTimeVerified=false`, `publicationLagStatus=UNCONFIRMED`, and
`historicalVintageVerified=false` remain separate from no-lookahead correctness.
